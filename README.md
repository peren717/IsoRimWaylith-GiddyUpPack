# IsoRim Waylith - Giddy-Up Pack

[Giddy-Up 2 - Continued](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3674332861) riders in the 3D view of [IsoRim Waylith](https://github.com/peren717/IsoRim-1.7) (RimWorld 1.7).

- Riders sit on their mounts and move with them.
- Riders turn to aim and shoot.
- With IsoRimZ: mounts stay on the rider's floor.

Requires IsoRim Waylith and Giddy-Up 2 - Continued. Also supports [IsoRimZ](https://github.com/peren717/IsoRim-ProjectZ) (optional, multiple floors). Load after IsoRim Waylith and Giddy-Up 2.

Giddy-Up 2 - Continued is made by Roolo, Owlchemist, dav9670 and Meme Goddess. This pack only shows its riders in 3D and contains no files of the original mod.
